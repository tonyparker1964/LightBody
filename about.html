About LightBody

LightBody is an interactive visual meditation on the connection between energy, the human form, and illuminated pathways. A constellation of 114 symbolic energy points gradually awakens across a luminous silhouette.

The experience

Activate the points in sequence, pause to explore the pattern, adjust the speed and radiance, or reveal the full illuminated figure. Stylized neural pathways, arteries, and veins weave through the visualization to express the relationship between physical anatomy and symbolic energy imagery.

Art and science

The 114-point map is a symbolic creative framework, not a scientifically established map of chakras. The illustrated blood vessels and nerves are stylized, not intended for medical or anatomical instruction.
